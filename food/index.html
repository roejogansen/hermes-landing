Questions.

Why Telegram?

You already check it. No new app to install, no new habit to build. Your nutritionist lives where your conversations are.

Does it work for families?

Yes. Tell Hermes who eats dinner together and it scales recipes accordingly. Allergies in the household? Handled. Only your portion gets tracked.

What if I go off-plan?

Nothing happens. Seriously. Had pizza? Great. Hermes notes it, moves on, and adjusts next week's plan if it sees a pattern. No guilt, no "you have 200 calories left."

How is this different from MyFitnessPal?

MyFitnessPal is a database you search. Hermes is a nutritionist you text. It plans, you eat. The entire point is that you spend less time thinking about food, not more.

Is it available in my country?

Launching in Norway first — grocery price comparison works with Norwegian stores. Meal planning and tracking work anywhere.
